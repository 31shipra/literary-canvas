Poetry

Words that linger.

Poems for quiet mornings, restless nights, and all the feelings that are difficult to put into ordinary sentences.

Between Yesterday and Tomorrow

There is a place between yesterday and tomorrow where the heart rests without asking what comes next. A quiet room, a half-open window, the soft memory of rain. Here, we are neither leaving nor arriving. We simply breathe and remember that becoming takes time.

When the Evening Comes

When the evening comes, let the unfinished things remain unfinished. Let the words you could not say wait until morning. Not everything needs an ending tonight. Some stories need darkness before they understand how to become light.
